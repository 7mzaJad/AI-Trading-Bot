AI Trading Bot - Future Enhancements & Expansion Possibilities

1. Dynamic Stock Screener
Instead of a hardcoded list of stocks, we can write a script that wakes up at 9:00 AM, scans the entire stock market for the top 10 most active/trending stocks of the day, and feeds only those to the AI.

2. Discord/Telegram Notifications
We can add a webhook so that every time the bot places a trade, it sends a push notification directly to your phone explaining what it bought, the confidence level, and the reasoning.

3. Alternative Data Sources
We can expand the data fetcher to scrape Reddit (WallStreetBets), Twitter/X sentiment, or read live Federal Reserve interest rate announcements to give the AI an informational edge over standard technical indicators.

4. Backtesting Engine
We can build a separate script that runs our exact AI strategy against historical market data to mathematically prove the win rate and how much money it would have made before risking real capital.

5. Advanced Portfolio Rebalancing
Currently, the bot buys stocks independently. We could add a portfolio manager that ensures no single sector (e.g., Tech vs. Gold) dominates the portfolio, automatically selling winners to rebalance the account.

6. Live Trading
Once we are completely confident in the paper trading results over a few weeks, we can simply flip the ALPACA_PAPER flag to 'False' in the .env file to allow the bot to execute trades with real money.
